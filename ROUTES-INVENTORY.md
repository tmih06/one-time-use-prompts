## Mismatches (all fixed; evidence in SEO-AUDIT-PROGRESS.md V1–V5)

1. No sitemap; and no `Sitemap:` line / robots.txt (P2, infra).
2. All 13 HTML pages share the title `One-Time-Use Prompts`; prompt, phase and purpose pages have no description (P1, infra: one layout).
3. No `<link rel="canonical">` anywhere; `og:image` is a relative URL, so scrapers cannot resolve it reliably (P1, infra).
4. No Twitter card tags, `og:url`, `og:description`, `og:site_name`; home and purpose pages have no `og:image` (P2).
5. No JSON-LD anywhere (P2).
6. `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (P3).
7. `/404` lacks `noindex` (P3). `.md` twins and `/api/search` unmarked (P2).
8. Gallery filtering rewrites the URL with `pushState` but not `document.title` (P3, client only).
